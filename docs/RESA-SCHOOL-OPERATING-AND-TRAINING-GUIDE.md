# RESA Digital Campus — School Operating & Training Guide

**School:** RHEMA EXPLOITS SCIENCE ACADEMY INT'L  
**Session:** 2026/2027  
**Prepared:** 20 November 2026  
**Document status:** Training draft for controlled school review. Confirm menu labels and permissions against the live portal before using this as a formal operating procedure.

## 1. Safe operating rules

1. Use each person's own RESA account. Never share administrator credentials or use another person's account to perform an approval.
2. Confirm the school, academic session, term, learner identity and class before saving any record.
3. Verify a person's identity and role before approving an invitation or linking a parent to a learner.
4. Do not create a duplicate learner or staff record to work around a failed link. Record the error and ask the administrator to investigate.
5. Treat a payment as pending until the Finance Office verifies it in RESA. A transfer screenshot or checkout attempt is not proof of payment and is not an official receipt.
6. Do not publish an examination, result, assignment or announcement until the content, audience and dates have been checked.
7. Do not upload confidential learner information to a public link. Use the approved RESA portal and school-only visibility where appropriate.
8. Report suspected account compromise, incorrect access, missing records or unexpected notifications immediately.

## 2. Administrator training

### A. Start-of-term setup
- Sign in with the administrator account and confirm the school name, current academic session and term.
- Check that the required classes, subjects and staff records exist before assigning learners or teachers.
- Check that each staff member has the correct role and active school membership.
- Confirm the school contact information and customer-care escalation route.

### B. Admissions and enrolment
1. Open Admissions Management and locate the application.
2. Match the applicant to the submitted form and supporting documents.
3. Check name, date of birth, parent/guardian contact, requested class and form-payment verification.
4. Approve or reject only after the school's normal review.
5. After approval, open Students and verify that the learner record is present.
6. Open Class Enrolments; select the correct learner, academic session and class; save.
7. Re-open the learner record and verify the enrolment. Do not create a second learner if the link is missing.

### C. Staff invitations and teaching assignments
1. Open Staff Invitations and invite the staff member using the verified email address and intended role.
2. Confirm the person and role before approving access.
3. Open Classes & Subjects / teaching assignments.
4. Assign the correct class, subject and academic session. Set a form-teacher relationship only when authorised.
5. Ask the teacher to sign in and confirm the correct dashboard and assigned classes appear.

### D. Form-teacher assignment and attendance
1. Open **Form Teacher & Attendance** as an administrator.
2. Select the intended teacher, class and academic session; assign the form teacher and verify the assignment appears in the list.
3. Confirm the teacher signs in and sees the correct class/session in the form-teacher attendance workspace.
4. Assign only the teacher approved by school management. Do not assume that a subject teacher is automatically the form teacher.

### E. Attendance
- Open the attendance workspace for the correct date and session.
- Confirm the selected class or staff group before recording attendance.
- Use the approved biometric/camera/card workflow available at the school. Use the documented fallback process when a card is missing or a device is unavailable.
- Correct an attendance error through the authorised correction workflow and retain the audit trail.
- Review student and staff attendance separately.
- Check parent notification preferences and escalation procedures. Do not promise a push alert until a real device test confirms delivery.

### F. Results and examinations
- Confirm the session, term, class and subject before entering or importing marks.
- Check score totals, grading rules and learner identity before publishing.
- Preview the result and confirm the intended audience before publishing.
- For CBT, verify the examination instructions, schedule, duration, class/session assignment and attempt rules before publishing.
- Confirm the examination is visible to an enrolled learner account before the examination window begins.
- Never edit a live examination or result as a workaround for an access problem; record the issue and use the authorised correction process.

### G. Fees and payment verification
1. Review fee types and the correct learner/session assignments.
2. Check each learner's amount due, paid amount and balance.
3. For bank transfer or office payment, verify the transaction using the school's approved Finance evidence and bank records.
4. Record/confirm payment only through the authorised Finance workflow.
5. Confirm the balance changes only after verification and that the official receipt is generated.
6. Reconcile the payment record against the bank statement. Escalate mismatched amounts or duplicate references.
7. The Paystack option is currently marked **Coming soon — not activated**. Do not instruct parents to use it or imply that online checkout is live.

### H. RESA Drive, assignments and communication
- Upload only approved school materials and choose the intended visibility.
- After upload, verify that the file appears in the list and opens for an authorised user.
- Teachers should publish assignments to the intended class and verify due dates and instructions.
- Students should submit the correct file and confirm the submission status.
- Teachers should mark submissions and publish feedback; students should confirm the feedback is visible.
- Use official School Messages/RESA chat channels for school communication. Avoid sending sensitive student information to an unverified recipient.

## 3. Teacher training

1. Sign in using your own teacher account and check your name, dashboard and assigned classes.
2. Confirm the correct session, class and subject before taking attendance or entering marks.
3. Take attendance only for the class and date assigned to you. Form-teacher attendance access is enabled only after the administrator assigns you to the correct class and academic session. Report missing learners or incorrect class lists to the administrator.
4. Publish lesson materials only to the intended class and verify that learners can open them.
5. Create assignments with clear instructions, allowed file types and a realistic due date.
6. Check that the assignment is visible to the intended learners.
7. Review submissions, mark them using the school's grading guidance, and provide constructive feedback.
8. Enter results carefully, check totals and submit for the required administrative review/publication.
9. Use RESA chat and announcements professionally. Do not share account passwords or confidential records in group messages.
10. Report stale unread counts, missing messages, failed uploads or incorrect access with the time, page and a screenshot that contains no passwords or sensitive personal data.

## 4. Parent/guardian training

### Parent phone-notification setup
1. Open the RESA portal over HTTPS on the Android phone using a supported browser.
2. Sign in to the correct parent account and open **Notification Settings**.
3. Turn on phone notifications and accept the browser permission prompt.
4. Confirm the portal reports that the device is registered. If permission was previously blocked, change the browser's site notification permission in Android settings and try again.
5. Keep the in-portal inbox as the authoritative fallback until the school completes a real test notification and confirms it appears on the phone.
6. Report missing alerts to the school administrator; do not assume the preference toggle alone means the phone has subscribed.

2. Confirm that the correct learner(s) are linked to your account. If a child is missing or incorrectly linked, contact the school; do not create another account.

## 5. Student training

1. Sign in using your own student account.
2. Confirm your name, class and current session. Tell the school if any detail is wrong.
3. Check announcements, lesson materials, assignments and published examinations.
4. Submit assignments before the due date and confirm the portal shows the submission.
5. For CBT, read the instructions, confirm the correct exam and submit answers before the timer ends. Do not refresh, close or switch devices during an active exam unless instructed by the school.
6. Review published results and teacher feedback. Raise a query through the approved school channel if something appears incorrect.
7. Keep your password private and sign out on shared devices.

## 6. Customer Care and incident reporting

For every report, capture:
- Date and local time (Africa/Lagos)
- User role (do not include passwords or access tokens)
- Page/feature and action attempted
- Exact error text
- Device, browser and network type
- Whether the issue affects one account or multiple accounts
- A redacted screenshot where useful

Escalate suspected unauthorised access, incorrect learner-parent links, unexpected data visibility, payment discrepancies, missing examination submissions and attendance integrity issues to the administrator immediately.
