Introducing RESA Digital Campus

Less scattered paperwork. More connected school operations.

RESA Digital Campus brings key school-management workflows together in one online portal concept. The platform is being developed and refined around practical school needs, with implementation and feature availability confirmed during each demonstration.

♙

People & school records

Organise student, parent, teacher and staff information, enrolment workflows, classes and role-based access.

◷

Attendance workflows

Explore attendance tools, QR and device-assisted workflows, check-in records and parent notification concepts.

▤

Learning & examinations

Bring computer-based tests, examination publishing, assignments, practice activities and results into the school workflow.

₦

Fees & finance workflows

Support fee schedules, payment tracking, verification steps and receipt workflows with school-controlled processes.

⌂

Parent & student access

Give families and learners a dedicated place to follow relevant school information and published updates.

✉

Communication & notices

Explore announcements, messaging and notification workflows that help connect school communities.

▦

Smart school calendar

Plan normal school days, holidays, closures and authorised special activity days with clear operating schedules.

▧

Reports & administration

Bring operational summaries and administrative tools together to support day-to-day decision-making.

✧

Digital assistance & maps

Make it easier to find guidance, navigate school information and access location and direction support.

Honest product information: RESA Digital Campus is an evolving platform. Some capabilities may be in development, under testing or dependent on configuration. A demonstration will identify what is currently available, what requires setup and what is planned. No school records or private student information are displayed on this promotional site.
