Product overview

RESA Digital Campus

A connected digital experience for modern school operations.

RESA Digital Campus is a school-management platform initiative designed to bring important administration, learning, communication and operational workflows into one online environment.

What the platform is designed to support

People & enrolment

Student, parent, teacher and staff records, enrolment workflows, classes and role-based access.

Attendance workflows

Attendance records, QR and device-assisted workflows, check-in support and parent notification concepts.

Learning & examinations

Computer-based tests, examinations, assignments, practice activities and results workflows.

Fees & finance

Fee schedules, payment tracking, verification steps and receipt workflows.

Parent & student portals

Dedicated access to relevant school information, learner updates and published notices.

Communication

Announcements, messaging and notification workflows for school communities.

Smart school calendar

Normal school days, holidays, closures and authorised special activity schedules.

Reports & digital assistance

Administrative summaries, guidance, maps and navigation support.

Who can benefit?

Nursery, primary and secondary schools; colleges and training centres; school proprietors and administrators; education organisations; and institutions exploring practical digital transformation.
